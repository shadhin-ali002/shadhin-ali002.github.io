About

My training is in biochemistry and molecular biology, and most of my time is spent at the bench. I've used multiplex and species-specific PCR to check what's really inside processed meat products sold in Bangladesh. I've also extracted and quality-checked genomic DNA from human whole blood, the step that every PCR, sequencing or mutation analysis depends on.

I think routine techniques still need discipline. Primer design, reaction setup and gel reading each decide whether a result can be trusted. I'm now adding data analysis to my lab work, and I'm especially interested in molecular diagnostics, food authenticity, mitochondrial DNA and antimicrobial resistance.

Research

Food safety

Meat species authentication

Used multiplex PCR and species-specific PCR on DNA from retail processed foods in Bangladesh to detect adulteration and measure how much meat had been substituted. Published in Food Safety and Risk.

Genomics

Genomic DNA from whole blood

Extracted genomic DNA, including mitochondrial DNA, from human whole blood with spin-column kits, then checked yield and purity (A260/A280) on a NanoDrop to make it ready for PCR and sequencing.

Spin-column extraction · NanoDrop · DNA QC

Methods

Conventional PCR workflow

Ran routine conventional PCR from reaction setup and primer choice (for example goat 16S rRNA) through thermal cycling to checking the products on agarose gels.

PCR · Primer design · Gel electrophoresis

Bioinformatics

AMR analysis for N. gonorrhoeae

An open-source Python tool for gonococcal antimicrobial resistance. It summarises MIC workbooks (E-strip vs agar dilution agreement, QC) and finds mutations in 30 resistance genes from genome assemblies with BLAST+. Results come out as Excel tables and PDF/HTML reports.

Python · BLAST+ · MIC analysis
